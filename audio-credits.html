The blessings you hear

The recitation

Vivāha Maṅgalāṣṭakam, verses 1–2, recited by Prathosh A P. From the Vāgdhenu Sanskrit Chant Corpus, original files mangala__mangala_1.wav and mangala__mangala_2.wav (style A).

Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. Two complete verses have been selected, level-balanced, joined with a short pause and mixed with original accompaniment. The reciter’s inclusion does not imply endorsement of this invitation.

The shankh

“Snailhorn.wav” by Ohrwurm, released under CC0 / Public Domain. A short excerpt is used with reduced volume, gentle fades and AAC conversion.

The accompaniment and meanings

The quiet accompaniment is an original arrangement with a warm tonal bed, softly plucked strings and a brief flute response between verses. The voice remains in the foreground. The complete recitation plays once per visit. Afterwards, only a separate, quiet instrumental arrangement continues, and it can be paused at any time.

The English and Hindi captions are brief paraphrases: a blessing for an auspicious beginning and a prayer for auspiciousness each day. They are not a complete word-for-word translation.
